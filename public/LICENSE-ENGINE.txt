────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. CONFORMANCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Only seals produced in exact conformance with PSI-SEAL/1 — carrying the
schema identifier and matching schema digest, and byte-exact under RFC 8785
canonicalisation — are PSI-compliant. Outputs that deviate, even if
functionally similar, WILL fail verification. Presenting non-conformant
output as PSI-compliant is misrepresentation and infringement.
